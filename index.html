                  Matter Types

                  A matter type is a kind of work — Criminal Defense, Civil, Prosecution, Appellate. It pre-fills a new matter's caption authority and which signing profile signs; the matter can change either. It does not decide where files are saved: those go under the client. A city, township or business you work for is set up as an Organization client on the People tab, with its own caption name and signing profile, not as a matter type.

                  Letterheads

                  Used by letters and memoranda — the masthead, address and seal printed at the top of the page. Everything here prints exactly as typed. The reference initials separator is deliberately different between letterheads: a colon on city letterhead, a slash on personal.

                  Backup

                  Your matters and people are stored on this computer only. The app keeps automatic backups, but those sit on the same drive — use Back Up Now to save a copy somewhere else, such as OneDrive or a USB stick.
